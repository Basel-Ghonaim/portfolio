# 01 — Goals and Audience

**Status:** Approved
**Approved on:** 2026-10-22

## Decisions

| ID | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| D1-01 | The portfolio serves a balanced, simple mix of three goals: helping the owner get employment, attracting freelance clients, and building a public professional presence. | A personal portfolio must support all three without becoming complex. |
| D1-02 | Sharing on social media is a core requirement. Every public page provides an appropriate share preview (title, description, image) in its language. | Links are mainly distributed by the owner through social platforms, applications, and messages. |
| D1-03 | One clear experience serves all visitor types: technical and non-technical recruiters, engineers, and project owners. There are no separate paths per audience. | Keeps the product simple while remaining useful to every expected visitor. |
| D1-04 | English is the primary language of the project. Content is divided into bilingual content and English-only content. Detailed classification is decided in axis 06. | English is the norm in the technical field; full bilingual parity for all content adds cost without matching value. |
| D1-05 | Search visibility: basic technical readiness from the start (stable URLs, per-page titles and descriptions, sitemap, exclusion of drafts and the dashboard, semantic structure). No search content strategy, keyword optimization, or ranking monitoring. | Visitors arrive mainly through links the owner shares. The basics are cheap to build early, expensive to retrofit, and keep the door open for future evolution. |
| D1-06 | Professional positioning: "Frontend-focused Full-Stack Developer". Backend work is highlighted in projects as evidence. The positioning text is editable from the dashboard. | Honest positioning sets correct expectations for technical reviewers while signaling end-to-end capability to clients. It can evolve as backend experience grows. |
| D1-07 | The primary call to action is "Contact me". CV download remains visible but secondary. | Direct contact serves all three goals; the CV mainly serves employment. |
| D1-08 | The first release is a portfolio, not a platform. Decisions that would block future evolution into a platform are avoided. | Avoids premature complexity without closing future options. |
